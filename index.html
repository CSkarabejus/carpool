📲 How to Install SplitRide

🤖 On your Poco X4 Pro (Android)

  1. Open this page in Chrome (or MIUI browser).
  2. Tap the 3 vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Tap "Add" / "Install". The app icon will appear directly on your phone's home screen and app drawer!

💻 On your Mac (Safari / Chrome)

In Safari: In the top Mac menu bar, click File ➔ "Add to Dock...".

In Chrome: Click the 3 dots (⋮) ➔ "Save and share" ➔ "Install SplitRide..." (or look for the install icon on the right side of the URL bar).
